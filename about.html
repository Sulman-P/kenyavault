Our Story

KenyaVault was founded with a simple mission: to make quality educational resources accessible to every Kenyan educator and learner. We understand the challenges teachers face in preparing comprehensive lesson plans, schemes of work, and assessments that align with the CBC (Competency-Based Curriculum) framework. Our team consists of experienced educators, curriculum specialists, and technology professionals dedicated to serving Kenyan schools.

Our platform brings together a curated collection of educational materials created by experienced educators, reviewed for quality, and made available at affordable prices. From Preprimary through University education, we provide resources that save time and enhance teaching effectiveness. All content is aligned with KICD (Kenya Institute of Curriculum Development) standards and revised regularly to ensure accuracy and relevance.

We believe that when educators have access to excellent teaching materials, students receive better education. That's why we're committed to continuously expanding our library and ensuring every resource meets the highest standards — whether you're teaching Preprimary, Primary, Junior Secondary (Grade 7–9), Senior Secondary (Grade 10–12), or preparing for KJSEA 2026 and KCSE.
