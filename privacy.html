1. Introduction & Scope

KenyaVault Academy ("we," "our," "us") is a complete learning ecosystem that provides interactive digital content, KICD-aligned CBC resources, and student-centric support for learners, teachers, and parents across Kenya.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your personal information when you use our platform (kenyavault.co.ke) and our services, including:

  • Learners (Students): Accessing interactive lessons, revision materials, and practice content
  • Teachers: Using lesson plans, schemes of work, and assessment tools
  • Parents & Guardians: Supporting their children's learning journey
  • Institutional Buyers: Schools and educational institutions making bulk purchases

We are committed to protecting your privacy and handling your personal information with transparency and care. This policy complies with the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), and the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) where applicable.

📌 Key Principle: We collect only the information necessary to provide you with our educational services. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties for marketing purposes.

📜 References: Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019 (Sections 25-31); ODPC Guidelines on Data Protection for Educational Institutions

2. Lawful Processing of Personal Data

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act (2019), we are required to have a lawful basis for processing your personal data. KenyaVault Academy processes personal data under the following legal bases:

  • Consent: When you voluntarily provide your information to create an account, make a purchase, or subscribe to our services, you give us explicit consent to process your data for those purposes
  • Contractual Necessity: Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract with you, such as delivering educational resources you have purchased
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject (e.g., tax and accounting requirements)
  • Legitimate Interest: Processing is necessary for our legitimate interests in providing and improving our educational services, provided that such interests are not overridden by your data protection rights
🔍 For Students and Parents:
  • When you sign up as a learner or parent, we rely on your consent and parental consent where applicable
  • We collect data about your learning progress and resource usage to personalize your experience and improve our educational content
  • You have the right to withdraw your consent at any time by contacting us

📜 References: Kenya DPA Section 26 (Lawful Processing); ODPC Guidance on Consent

3. Information We Collect

3.1 Information You Provide

When you use KenyaVault Academy, we may collect the following information that you voluntarily provide:

  • Account Registration Details: Full name, email address, phone number, and role (Student, Teacher, Parent, School Administrator) when you create an account
  • Student Information: Grade level, subjects of interest, and learning preferences to personalize educational content
  • School or Institutional Information: School name, address, and official contact details for institutional buyers
  • Payment Information: M-PESA transaction codes and payment references (we do not store your M-PESA PIN or full payment credentials)
  • Learning Progress: Data on resources viewed, completed lessons, quiz scores, and learning milestones to track progress and improve recommendations
  • Communications: Messages, inquiries, and feedback you send to us via WhatsApp, email, or our contact forms

3.2 Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our Site, we automatically collect certain information about your device and usage:

  • IP Address & Device Type: To prevent fraudulent activity, detect suspicious behavior, and ensure fair usage of our resources
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, resources viewed, search queries, and click patterns to improve user experience
  • Download History: Records of resources you have downloaded to enable your dashboard history and maintain access to purchased content
  • Learning Analytics: Data on learning progress, completion rates, and engagement metrics to personalize recommendations
  • Cookies: Small text files stored on your device to enhance your experience and remember your preferences
🔒 We do not collect:
  • Sensitive personal information (e.g., race, religion, health data, political opinions)
  • Financial account numbers, passwords, or full payment credentials
  • Biometric data or precise location tracking

📜 References: Kenya DPA Section 2 (Definitions); ODPC Data Protection Principles

4. How We Use Your Data

We use the information we collect for the following specific purposes related to our educational platform:

  • Learning Experience: To personalize educational content based on grade level, subjects of interest, and learning progress
  • Progress Tracking: To track learning milestones, completion rates, and achievements to provide meaningful feedback and recommendations
  • Dashboard Management: To provide you with a personalized dashboard showing your learning journey, purchased resources, and progress
  • Delivery of Digital Assets: To process your purchases and deliver downloadable educational resources to your dashboard or email
  • Customer Support: To respond to your inquiries, resolve issues related to resource access, and provide technical assistance
  • Platform Improvement: To analyze usage patterns, identify popular resources, and enhance the overall learning experience
  • Communication: To send order confirmations, payment receipts, download links, and important updates about our services
  • Legal Compliance: To comply with applicable laws, regulations, and legal processes
  • Security: To detect and prevent fraud, unauthorized access, and other security issues
✅ Your Information, Your Control: We only use your information for the purposes explicitly stated in this policy. We do not use your data for marketing without your explicit consent.

📜 References: Kenya DPA Section 26 (Purpose of Processing); ODPC Data Processing Guidelines

5. Secure Payment Processing

KenyaVault Academy prioritizes the security of your financial transactions. We utilize industry-standard, third-party payment gateways to process all payments securely:

  • Local Mobile Money: M-PESA STK push transactions are processed through trusted payment gateways such as Megapay or Safaricom's official APIs. We do not store your M-PESA PIN or any sensitive financial credentials
  • Credit/Debit Cards: Card payments are processed through encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant gateways like Stripe or Pesapal. All card details are handled by the payment processor and are never stored on our servers
  • Data Encryption: All payment data is transmitted using SSL/TLS encryption, ensuring that your financial information is protected during the transaction process
  • No Raw Financial Data: KenyaVault Academy does not store raw billing or financial data. We retain only transaction references, order histories, and payment confirmations necessary for record-keeping and customer support
🔒 PCI DSS Compliant: Our payment processors adhere to the Payment Card Industry Data Security Standard (PCI DSS), ensuring the highest level of security for your payment information.

📜 References: Kenya DPA Section 26(2) (Data Security); PCI DSS Requirements 3.1-3.4

6. Progress Tracking & Dashboard Data

As an educational platform, KenyaVault Academy collects and manages learning progress data to enhance the learning experience:

  • Learning Progress: We track which resources you have accessed, lessons completed, and learning milestones achieved to provide personalized recommendations and celebrate your progress
  • Dashboard Personalization: Your dashboard displays your learning journey, including purchased resources, progress tracking, and suggested next steps based on your learning patterns
  • Performance Insights: For students, we may provide insights into learning strengths and areas for improvement based on engagement with educational content
  • Data Ownership: You have full access to your progress data and can request its deletion at any time
  • No Third-Party Sharing: Your learning progress data is not shared with third parties for marketing or any other purpose without your explicit consent
📊 Your Learning Dashboard: Your progress data is stored securely and is only used to enhance your learning experience. You can view, export, or delete your progress data at any time through your account settings.

📜 References: Kenya DPA Section 26 (Purpose of Processing); ODPC Data Subject Rights

7. Third-Party Data Sharing

We share your personal data with third parties only when necessary to provide our services. All third-party processors are bound by strict data protection agreements:

  • Payment Processors: We share transaction information with Megapay, Stripe, and Pesapal to process payments. These processors are PCI-DSS compliant and do not retain your data beyond what is necessary for transaction processing
  • Hosting & Infrastructure: We use Supabase as our database and hosting provider. Data is encrypted at rest and in transit
  • Communication Services: We use email and SMS services to send order confirmations, receipts, and important updates
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how users interact with our platform. Analytics data is anonymized and does not identify you personally
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose your data when required by law, court order, or government regulation to comply with legal obligations
🔒 Your Data is Safe:
  • We never sell, rent, or trade your personal data to third parties
  • All third-party processors are contractually bound to protect your data
  • We only share the minimum amount of data necessary for each service

📜 References: Kenya DPA Section 30 (Data Controller Responsibilities); ODPC Guidance on Data Processing Agreements

KenyaVault Academy is committed to protecting the privacy of children and young learners. This section explains how we handle data for students in simple, easy-to-understand language:

🌟 For Students (A Message for You)

  • We collect your data to help you learn: We use your name, grade level, and learning progress to personalize your learning experience and show you the right resources
  • We keep your data safe: We use strong security measures to protect your information, just like we protect our own
  • You can ask us questions: If you ever have questions about your privacy, you can ask your parent, teacher, or contact us directly
  • We don't sell your data: Your information is never sold to anyone. It's only used to help you learn
  • You have the right to privacy: If you don't want us to use your data anymore, you or your parent can ask us to delete it

  • Parental Monitoring: We encourage parents to monitor their children's online activities and discuss the importance of privacy and online safety
  • Parental Rights: Parents and guardians have the right to access, correct, or delete their child's personal data at any time
  • Educational Content: All resources on our platform are designed for educational purposes and are appropriate for school-age children under adult supervision
👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Parental Guidance: We recommend that parents and educators review resources before children access them to ensure age-appropriateness and alignment with educational goals.

📜 References: Kenya DPA Section 30 (Children's Data); UN Convention on the Rights of the Child; ODPC Guidelines on Protection of Children's Data

9. Data Retention & User Rights

We retain your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy, including:

  • Transaction Records: We retain purchase records for tax and accounting purposes as required by Kenyan law (typically 7 years) and to ensure that you can access your purchased resources at any time
  • Learning Progress: Your progress data is retained for as long as your account is active to provide continuous learning support
  • Download History: Download history is stored for user retrieval to ensure that teachers and schools don't lose access to their bought resources
  • Account Information: If you create an account, we retain your information until you request deletion or the account becomes inactive
  • Analytics Data: Aggregated usage data may be retained indefinitely for analytics and improvement purposes (this data does not identify you personally)

Your Rights Under Kenya DPA, GDPR & CCPA

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the following rights regarding your personal information:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the personal information we hold about you
  • Right to Rectification: You can correct inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Right to Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): You can request that we delete your personal information
  • Right to Restrict Processing: You can limit how we use your information
  • Right to Data Portability: You can request your data in a structured, machine-readable format
  • Right to Object: You can object to the processing of your data for certain purposes
  • Right to Withdraw Consent: You can withdraw your consent at any time
📧 To exercise any of these rights: Contact our Data Protection Officer at adminnexalearn@gmail.com with your request. We will respond within 30 days as required by law.

📜 References: Kenya DPA Sections 26-30; GDPR Articles 15-21; ODPC Data Subject Rights Guidelines

10. Data Security Measures

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement industry-standard measures to protect it:

  • Encryption: All data transmitted between your browser and our Site is encrypted using SSL/TLS technology. Data at rest is encrypted using AES-256 encryption
  • Access Controls: Only authorized personnel with a legitimate need have access to personal information. All access is logged and monitored
  • Secure Storage: Your information is stored on secure servers with restricted access, firewalls, and intrusion detection systems
  • Regular Monitoring: We regularly monitor our systems for potential vulnerabilities, security threats, and unauthorized access attempts
  • Data Minimization: We collect only the minimum amount of information necessary to provide our services
  • Incident Response: We have established incident response procedures to quickly address any security breaches
  • Staff Training: All employees and contractors undergo regular privacy and security awareness training
⚠️ Important: While we strive to protect your information, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security, but we are committed to continuously improving our security measures.

📜 References: Kenya DPA Section 26(2) (Security Safeguards); ODPC Security Measures Guidelines
